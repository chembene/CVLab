# Pricing Strategy

## Objective

Create a pricing model that is simple, transparent, and affordable, while ensuring the long-term sustainability of the platform.

---

# Pricing Philosophy

Users should only pay when they receive value.

Credits are consumed only after successful processing.

No hidden costs.

No automatic charges.

---

# Initial Credit Packages

| Package | Price (MT) | Credits |
|----------|-----------:|--------:|
| Starter | 100 MT | 200 |
| Standard | 250 MT | 550 |
| Professional | 500 MT | 1,200 |

Larger packages provide bonus credits.

---

# Refund Policy

Credits are refunded automatically when:

- AI processing fails.
- Payment is reversed.
- Internal server errors occur after credit reservation.

Credits are never refunded after successful processing.

---

# Future Subscription Plans

Potential subscription plans:

- Free
- Professional
- Enterprise
- University
- NGO

Subscriptions may include monthly credits and premium features.

---

# Design Principles

Pricing should encourage long-term platform usage rather than one-time purchases.
