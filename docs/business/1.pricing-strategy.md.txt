# Pricing Strategy

## Objective

Create a pricing model that is simple, transparent, and affordable, while ensuring the long-term sustainability of the platform.

---

# Pricing Philosophy

Users should only pay when they receive value.

Credits are consumed only after successful processing.

No hidden costs.

No automatic charges.

## Interview Practice — per-mode breakdown (FASE 11)

Interview Practice was originally one combined 60-credit line item. FASE 11 (2026-08-12) split it by mode — each mode is a genuinely different amount of context for the AI to work with (CV + Job Target + ATS report vs. just a job vs. just a role/title), so each gets its own price, itemized as questions + evaluation exactly like the original combined figure was:

| Mode | Questions | Evaluation | Total |
|------|----------:|-----------:|------:|
| Resume (CV + Job Target) | 30 | 30 | 60 |
| Job (vaga, CV opcional) | 25 | 25 | 50 |
| Role (apenas cargo) | 20 | 20 | 40 |

Resume keeps the original 60-credit total unchanged. Job and Role are priced lower, reflecting less context available to the AI (no ATS report, and Role has neither a résumé nor a Job Target at all).

## Export Engine (Export Engine phase)

Resume export (PDF or DOCX) is not an AI feature — no provider call, no AI-generated content, deterministic generation of the user's own already-stored content. Priced separately, per document format:

| Format | Credits |
|--------|--------:|
| PDF | 10 |
| DOCX | 5 |

These are the values explicitly approved for this phase — see [export-engine.md](../architecture/export-engine.md) §7.

**Known discrepancy, deliberately not resolved here**: `prompts/11-credit-system.md` §3 (a CLAUDE.md-mandatory "authoritative project rule" file) lists different credit values than this document for several already-shipped features — e.g. Cover Letter Generation is 40 here but 25 in `prompts/11-credit-system.md`. That file also lists "PDF premium export: 10 credits" (matching the value used here) but says nothing about DOCX. This phase did **not** silently reconcile any of those pre-existing mismatches — only added the two new Export Engine rows above, using the values explicitly approved for this phase. Reconciling `prompts/11-credit-system.md` against what is actually implemented/seeded remains an open item for a future phase.

# Refund Policy

Credits are refunded automatically when:

- AI processing fails.
- Payment is reversed.
- Internal server errors occur after credit reservation.

Credits are never refunded after successful processing.
